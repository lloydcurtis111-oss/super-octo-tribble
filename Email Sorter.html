Email Sorter

Finds the brand deals, licensing offers and press requests buried in a flooded inbox, and helps you reply to them.

1. Get your email from Google

  1. Open takeout.google.com and sign in.
  2. Click Deselect all, then scroll down and tick Mail.
  3. Click Next step, then Create export.
  4. Google emails you when it's ready (a few minutes to a day). Download the .zip file. No need to unzip it.

2. Choose the file and sort

or drag the file here · a .zip or .mbox from Google Takeout
🔒 Your emails never leave this computer. Everything happens inside this page, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Tip: click Save reply page for later so you can come back to your replies without sorting again. It saves to your Downloads folder.
